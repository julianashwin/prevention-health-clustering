# measuring_health

The measurement write-ups and their figures and tables.

- `health_measure_construction.tex` — **the empirical construction**: Part I the
  health measure (with an opening section for an economics reader and the
  relation to the literature), Part II the cost index. Bibliography in
  `references.bib` (natbib). Compile: pdflatex, bibtex, pdflatex twice.
- `health_measures_note.tex` — the archived measure search and the earlier model
  generations (sections 9–12 hold the Bayesian results on the archived measures).
- `archive/` — `cost_proxy_plan.tex`, now folded into Part II of the construction
  note, and the pre-merge construction note.
- `figures/`, `tables/` — written by the scripts under `descriptives/`.
