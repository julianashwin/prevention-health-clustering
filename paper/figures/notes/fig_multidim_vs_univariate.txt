Three K = 3 AR(1) + transitory fits with one level shift per birth decade common to the classes, all drawn at the 1950s: theta alone on the health contract (38,963 people), the mental GRM alone on the multidim contract (38,181 people), and the multidimensional fit of theta, the mental GRM and Gompertz-Makeham mortality on the multidim contract. Line width is proportional to the class share; the legend gives the share and the class persistence on that channel. (c), (d): rows are the multidimensional modal class, columns the univariate modal class, row-normalised, on the 38,181 and 38,181 people in both fits; classes ordered worst to best on each fit's anchor (the physical channel for the multidimensional fit).
