made by: descriptives/50_paper_windows_bayes.py --k 4

Each window refits the K = 4 AR(1) + transitory mixture on theta on the people observed at least three times inside it (its own contract and fit). Coloured lines: the window's class paths at the posterior mean over its own age range (tick at the start; line width proportional to the class share); grey: the lifecycle 20-90 fit. Panel (c): over pairs of people in both fits (a random 3,000), the correlation between the probability that the window fit puts the pair in the same class and the probability that the lifecycle fit does, sum_k p_ik p_jk under each; it needs no correspondence between the two sets of labels and is 1 whenever the two posteriors coincide (the adjusted Rand index of the modal classes is in the csv). Panels (d), (e): the worst class's posterior share of the person-waves observed at each age (five-year rolling mean), one line per window, against the lifecycle fit in grey; (f) the worst class's share of each window's people. Classes ordered worst to best by the class path's mean over the window. Windows whose chains split into modes are read over the chains that share the better mode: 70-90 over chains 1,4; 60-90 over chains 1,2,3.
